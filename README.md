## Third-Party Risk Assessment and Vendor Risk Management ##

**Project Overview**

This project demonstrates a practical approach to assessing cybersecurity risks associated with a third-party cloud storage provider.

The scenario involves BrightPath Digital Services Ltd., a fictional digital marketing company considering a cloud storage vendor to store customer documents.

The assessment focuses on identifying vendor security gaps, requesting appropriate evidence, evaluating responses, documenting risks, and recommending risk treatments.

**Project Objectives**

* Understand third-party cybersecurity risks.
* Conduct basic vendor security due diligence.
* Identify missing or insufficient security evidence.
* Evaluate vendor responses and supporting documentation.
* Create and maintain a vendor risk register.
* Recommend appropriate risk treatment actions.

**Assessment Areas**

The assessment considers:

* Information security policies
* Data encryption
* Multi-factor authentication
* Access control
* Backup and recovery
* Incident response
* Contractual security obligations
* Data protection responsibilities

**Identified Risks**

Three risks were documented:

1. Encryption-at-rest coverage not adequately verified.
2. Backup restoration capability not adequately verified.
3. Incident notification obligations not agreed in the contract.

The risks were assessed using qualitative likelihood and impact ratings: Low, Medium, and High.

**Tools and Deliverables**

* Excel: Vendor assessment and vendor risk register.
* Word: Third-party risk assessment case study.

**Skills Demonstrated**

* Third-party risk assessment
* Vendor due diligence
* Evidence evaluation
* Cybersecurity risk identification
* Qualitative risk assessment
* Risk treatment recommendations
* GRC documentation

**Important Disclaimer**

This is a fictional learning and portfolio project. The vendor responses and assessment scenario are simulated. No real vendor was assessed, no actual security documents were obtained, and no technical security testing was performed.

The project demonstrates an understanding of the assessment process rather than a real-world audit or certification.
